# Commodore 64 system ROMs — copyright notice

These files are the **original Commodore 64 ROMs**:

- `basic.bin`   — C64 BASIC V2 ROM
- `kernal.bin`  — C64 KERNAL ROM
- `chargen.bin` — C64 character generator ROM
- `dos1541.bin` — Commodore 1541 disk-drive DOS ROM

## Licensing status

These ROMs are **proprietary and copyrighted**. The rights are held by Commodore
Business Machines and its successors (today generally attributed to Cloanto /
C64 IP / Retro Games Ltd.). **There is no free or open-source license for them**,
and hosting them publicly is legally a gray area.

They are included here only for **private archival/emulation use in connection
with original hardware the operator owns**. They are *not* offered under any
license and should not be treated as freely redistributable.

## Clean-licensing alternative

The bundled emulator (vc64web/VirtualC64) ships with **open-source replacement
ROMs** and can boot without these files by enabling its `openROMS` mode. Using
those instead removes the copyright concern at the cost of slightly lower
compatibility with a few titles. To switch, load the emulator with `openROMS`
enabled and stop shipping this `roms/` folder.
