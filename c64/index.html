PEMA's C64 DISK DUMPS

About

Around 2015, I inherited a Commodore 64 along with around 500 floppy disks. This page is an archive of some of the more interesting disks I found. Most of these are compilation disks, containing a variety of demos, games, warez, danish software, and even some porn. For anyone interested, I used a XUM1541-II board alongside OpenCBM to dump these disk images.

The simplest way to run a program is to press the "Files" button on the disk image you are interested in, then click on the program in the list that you want to run. The emulator will do the rest for you. Alternatively, you can mount the entire disk image with the "Attach" button, then enter LOAD"$",8 followed by LIST to view the contents. To load a specific program, type LOAD"<program name>",8,1 followed by RUN.
